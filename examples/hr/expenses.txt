Travel and expenses policy

Submit every expense report within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of each receipt.
Reports that arrive later are only paid if your manager approves them.

On business trips, the meal allowance is 40 euros per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Book economy class for flights. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours.
Trains are preferred over flights for trips shorter than 4 hours.
