Leave policy

Every employee gets 25 vacation days per year. Vacation days are added on January 1.
Unused vacation days can be carried over, but they expire on March 31 of the following year.

You can take up to 3 sick days in a row without a doctor's note. From the fourth day, send a
doctor's note to HR.

Parents get 16 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken any time in the first two years.
